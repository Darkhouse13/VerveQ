Delete your account

Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can delete your VerveQ account and the data tied to it at any time.

In the Android app

  1. Open VerveQ, go to the Profile tab and tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email support@verveq.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my VerveQ account” and your username. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details (email and password) and every active session.
  • Your username, display name and settings.
  • Your ratings, rank history, achievements, streaks and game history.
  • Reminder sign-ups and notifications tied to your account or email.

What is kept

Records shared with other players, such as a duel you played against someone or a Weekend crew’s results, stay so their history still adds up. Your name is removed from them and they are no longer linked to anything that identifies you. Anonymous usage statistics that never identified you are not affected.

Questions

Contact support@verveq.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
