# Owner kit — teaser-card screen recording

**Owner time: ~20 minutes** (setup 5, two paths × 2 takes ~10, transfer 5).
**Needed by: week 0** (it's the proof beat in `wknd-count` and any
"it's real" moment; the campaign spine ships without it if it slips —
cadence never blocks on an owner-side piece).

The Home teaser card is the ONLY real product surface until the mode
ships, and that's the frame: pre-launch mystery + a waitlist you can
actually join. This is a recording of the real app — generative AI never
renders UI, so this capture is the only product proof allowed on screen.

## What to capture (two clips)

**Clip A — anonymous email join (the money path):**
1. Phone, portrait, incognito/private browser tab (so you're anonymous
   and the email form shows, not the one-tap button).
2. Go to `verveq.com` (clean URL, no query string — the address bar may
   end up in frame and must look clean).
3. Land on Home, let the teaser card render, hold ~2s still.
4. Slow scroll so the card fills the frame; hold ~2s.
5. Tap the email field, type a real-looking address (use a throwaway you
   control — it WILL join the real waitlist), tap **Join**.
6. Hold on "You're in." for a full 3 seconds. That's the shot.

**Clip B — signed-in one-tap (backup/variant):**
Same flow signed in with a username: card shows **Count me in** → tap →
"You're in." Hold 3s.

## Rules

- Real device screen recording (iOS/Android built-in recorder), 60fps if
  offered. NOT a browser devtools emulation — real chrome, real feel.
- Do Not Disturb ON — no notification banners in the take.
- Don't rehearse typing to perfection; one small correction mid-type
  reads human and is fine. A wrong final email is not (re-take).
- If the waitlist count line is visible under the button, great — leave
  it in frame. Do not screenshot-doctor anything.
